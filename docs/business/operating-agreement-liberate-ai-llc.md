## ARTICLE V — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**5.1 Profits and Losses.** All profits and losses of the Company shall be allocated 100% to the Member.

**5.2 Distributions.** Distributions shall be made at the sole discretion of the Member, subject to the Act's restrictions on distributions that would render the Company unable to pay its debts as they become due.

**5.3 Tax Treatment.** The Company shall be treated as a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes. All items of income, deduction, gain, loss, and credit shall be reported on the Member's personal tax return (IRS Form 1040, Schedule C).

**5.4 Tax Elections.** The Member may elect to have the Company taxed as an S-Corporation or C-Corporation by filing the appropriate forms with the IRS, if such election is determined to be advantageous.

**6.2 Assignment of Pre-Existing IP.** The Member hereby assigns to the Company all rights, title, and interest in the intellectual property described in the IP Assignment Agreement executed concurrently with this Operating Agreement, including the software project known as "Deep Agent Network" and all associated materials.

**6.3 Work Product.** Any work product created by employees, contractors, or agents of the Company shall be deemed "work made for hire" to the extent permitted by law, and all rights therein shall belong to the Company. Where "work made for hire" doctrine does not apply, such persons shall assign all rights to the Company pursuant to written agreements.

## ARTICLE VII — LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

**7.1 Limited Liability.** The Member shall not be personally liable for the debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a member.

**7.2 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify the Member against any losses, damages, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) incurred in connection with the Company's business, except for losses arising from the Member's fraud, willful misconduct, or gross negligence.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — DISSOLUTION

**8.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon:

- (a) The written decision of the Member to dissolve the Company;
- (b) The entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under the Act;
- (c) Any event that makes it unlawful to continue the Company's business.

**8.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Member shall wind up the Company's affairs, pay its debts and obligations, and distribute any remaining assets to the Member.

**9.2 Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Member.

**9.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement regarding the Company's governance and supersedes all prior agreements.
